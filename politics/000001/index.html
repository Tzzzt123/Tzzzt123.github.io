French politics report early december 2025

19th December 2025

How LFI is weathering the current storm

This article is the first real article on this blog. Furthermore, I apologize for the writing style, I wrote this article when I was very tired. I will first detail some background information, later touch on the events in an investigating commission and how it benefited La France Insoumise. Later, I will detail how the far right in France may lose traction and then also mention some stuff regarding probable information for the 2027 presidential runoff before discussing the newest farmer protests in France. I will later end the article with some information regarding the parisian mayoral elections.

The happenings in the investigating committee

LR (Conservatives) decided, with the help of UDR (far-right conservatives) and RN (far-right) to try to create an investigating committee to "Investigate the Links between LFI (Mélenchon's party) and Islamism". That first attempt has been rejected by the national assembly because it specifically attacks a party, and investigating committees may not target a specific party. They therefore decided to rename it "Investigating the links between political parties and Islamism". Later, they summoned a several of people of the French intelligence community to testify. The result of the reports they were presented with was that LFI has no links to Islamist terrorism, but that in the meanwhile, the far right (RN) has had members with links to terrorists, such as executives in LafargeHolcim (a French-Swiss cement company) which gave money to ISIS, as well as a Member of RN providing weapons to the terrorists for the 2015 attacks against HyperCacher (a big kosher store in Paris) to name a few. When they noticed that the investigative committee would backfire against the right, they made yet another, even bigger, mistake: They decided to summon Mélenchon himself to force him to testify to try to find any crumb to build their case. Later, Laurent Wauquiez, the LR-Politician who initiated the idea of this investigative committee, publicly admitted that the purpose was to "destroy LFI".

Mélenchon accepts getting summoned under the condition that it’s either on a Saturday or a Sunday to maximize viewership. The entire testification of Mélenchon happened on December 6th and was broadcast live on 5 TV channels. Mélenchon just shreds the entire case, gives the entire country a 2-hour-long history lesson reaching back centuries, and successfully answers the entire investigative committee to a point where even right-wing-to-far right TV pundit said the following: "Jean-Luc Mélenchon is a politician in a class of his own, with a level of erudition, culture, and mastery of history that sets him apart from his opponents with their limited culture." while a centrist pundit said "In terms of form, he [Mélenchon] scored points: a masterful presentation, confident erudition, references to the Enlightenment… In a political class that is 'intellectually lacking,' this stands out and raises questions." That entire investigative committee performance of Mélenchon was such a big thing that it was one of the main topics in all news for over a week. Many people now are convinced that Mélenchon, shall he access the Presidential run-off, will shred his opponent alive in a debate.

The mistakes and demasking of the far right

In the meanwhile, far right has had several disastrous things happening to them: Jordan Bardella (RN's likely Presidential candidate) was invited to a rather chill TV program, and there he was openly clowned on for bootlicking on Trump. His TV performance was rather disastrous. In the same days, a new scandal regarding Bardella came out: He is in trouble for having used 133'000 EUR of public funds for public speaking trainings (one may see that they were not that useful). He also, jointly with Le Pen, was invited to the US embassy, where he met Ambassador Charles Kushner. This made him seem to be a foreign puppet and was not a good look in a country like France, which prides itself on independence from the US. Furthermore, a day later, a Mediapart report proved that Bardella's chief of staff used to be a Member in "Égalité & Reconciliation" (Equality and Reconciliation), an openly antisemitic, negationnist and neonazi party led by Alain Soral (who has been sentenced by courts several times for antisemitism and negationnism).

Quick interlude regarding 2027 Presidential Election predictions

The aforementioned information gets more interesting when put into the context that, Alain Duhamel, the longest-lasting TV journalist in French history (he has interviewed every President from De Gaulle all the way to Macron), stated he that believes that the hypothesis of a Mélenchon-Bardella Runoff is "very possible".

Farmer's Protests

Parallelly to all of this, France has a new series of farmer's protests breaking out. French farmers mainly protest against the slaughter of all cows in herds which are infected by lumpy-skin-disease, the likely signing of the EU-Mercosur Free Trade Agreement, very low incomes and against the conditions which currently lead to one farmer suicide per day in France on average. Two farmer's unions lead this protest, Confédération Paysanne, a left-wing farmer's union and Coordination Rurale, a right-wing farmer's union. Macron's government has attempted resolving the farmer's protest with violence, even sending helicopters to try to suppress the protests and using copious amounts of tear gas. During these protests, something unusual happened: Coordination Rurale, which is right-wing and used to be close to right-wing parties, has had several members distancing itself from the RN and showing pro-LFI signs, with several occurrences of Coordination Rurale members making pro-LFI and anti-RN statements. Firstly, one of them in south-western France stated "The only one who has been defending us is Manon Meunier from LFI", then at a protest in Strasbourg where farmers led politicians (from all political orientations) speak, RN and LFI both were allowed to speak. RN had the idea of bringing staffers to later heckle LFI to make it seem like LFI was disliked by Farmers, but that severely backfired, when yet another farmer from Coordination Rurale said "If there is someone who defends farmers, it is the LFI MP Manon Meunier and surely not the RN!". Manon Meunier (LFI MP of the 3rd constituency of Haute-Vienne department) benefits from the fact that she already in July addressed the issue regarding the lumpy-skin-disease while everyone else didn't mention it at all. Thus, the farmer's protests in France currently seem to be much more beneficial to the left, while they seem to be hurting the far right.

The parisian drawback

The only significant drawback for LFI in December was that in the Paris local elections, where EELV (Greens) and PCF ("Communists") have decided to ally behind PS (Social Democrats) and run without LFI in the first round. But the effects of such an alliance will have to be seen at the ballot box in the first round. And it is clear that LFI will have sufficient support in the first round to maintain its candidacy in the Parisian runoff (you need at least 10% in the first round to be guaranteed to maintain your list in the runoff, and LFI always is polling above this threshold in the Paris local elections). This currently sends signs towards Paris moving to be a three way tossup between PS, LFI, and LR, with maybe HOR (centre-right of Macronism) entering the runoff as well. The situation around LR and HOR is very chaotic though, with people endorsing each other in betrayal of their previous partners regularly. Shall the runoff have 4 candidates, either of them can win the runoff. In this case, it's likely that the winning list in Paris will have to form a coalition with one of the other 3 lists getting seats in the runoff.
